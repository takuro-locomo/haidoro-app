# Anatomy asset provenance

All five images are downloaded factual textbook illustrations, not AI-generated. Each was inspected using view_image on 2026-09-07.

## gray409-shoulder.png

- Local path: /workspace/scratch/hydro-anatomy-assets/gray409-shoulder.png
- Dimensions: 733 x 1156 px.
- Content: Posterior trunk; left side retains superficial trapezius and latissimus dorsi; right side has superficial muscles removed, showing levator scapulae (historical label Levator Anguli Scapulae), rhomboideus minor/major, splenius and infraspinatus. Upper section is well suited to explaining that the inferior portion of levator scapulae lies deep to trapezius.
- Limits: Split-level dissection, not a simultaneous unoccluded view of both sides. Historical lettering and dense fiber lines; use an upper-body viewport or zoom for neck/shoulder explanations.
- Author: Henry Vandyke Carter, from Henry Gray, Anatomy of the Human Body, 20th edition (1918), plate 409.
- License: Public domain (PD-scan / PD-old-100-expired), verified on Wikimedia file page.
- Suggested credit: Henry Vandyke Carter / Gray's Anatomy (1918), plate 409. Public domain. Via Wikimedia Commons.

## openstax-back.png

- Local path: /workspace/scratch/hydro-anatomy-assets/openstax-back.png
- Dimensions: 1620 x 1600 px.
- Content: Posterior intrinsic back muscles; multifidus at medial lumbar spine, longissimus thoracis lateral to it, and iliocostalis farther lateral, with semispinalis, spinalis and small deep muscles also shown. ## openstax-thigh.png (region expansion)

- Local path: /workspace/scratch/hydro-anatomy-assets/openstax-thigh.png
- Dimensions: 873 x 942 px. Visually inspected 2026-09-07.
- Content: Anterior right thigh with vastus lateralis explicitly labeled, plus rectus femoris, vastus medialis, sartorius, adductor group, and tensor fascia latae; also pelvis/iliopsoas/QL context.
